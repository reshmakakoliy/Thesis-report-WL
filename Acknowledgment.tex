Before anything else, we thank the Almighty God. He gave us the health to keep
working, the patience to get through the slow days, and the strength to finish
what we started. His blessings were with us throughout these years of study,
and long before them.

Our supervisor, \textbf{Dr. Sajeeb Saha}, \textbf{Professor}, Department of
Computer Science and Engineering, Jagannath University, Dhaka, deserves more
thanks than we can easily put into words. He stayed with us from our first
rough plan to the last experiment, and no matter how many questions we
brought him, he never once made us feel we were taking up too much of his
time. Whenever the work lost its way, he helped us think clearly again and
gave us practical ways forward. When we needed data and had no means of
getting it, he helped us arrange the funding. Without his guidance, this
thesis would look very different, and we are truly grateful for everything he
put into it.

We are thankful to the teachers and staff of the Department of Computer
Science and Engineering, Jagannath University. Everything they taught us and
all the help they gave us over the years prepared us for this work. We also
thank the Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB) for providing the
hydrological and meteorological records this study is built on.

Most of all, we thank our parents. Their love, their sacrifices and their
quiet belief in us carried us through the hardest parts of this journey,
often without them knowing how much it mattered. This achievement is as much
theirs as ours, and we dedicate it to them.
